## Key Concepts

### What is a SOC?

A Security Operations Center (SOC) is a centralized team responsible for continuously monitoring, detecting, investigating, and responding to cybersecurity threats. SOC analysts work together to protect an organization's systems, networks, and data from malicious activity.

---

### SOC Roles

The room introduced the different roles found within a SOC environment, including:

- Tier 1 SOC Analyst (Monitoring & Alert Triage)
- Tier 2 SOC Analyst (Incident Investigation)
- Tier 3 SOC Analyst (Threat Hunting & Advanced Analysis)
- SOC Manager
- Incident Response Team
- Threat Intelligence Team

Each role contributes to maintaining the organization's overall security posture.

---

### Incident Response Lifecycle

I learned the six major phases of incident response:

1. Preparation
2. Identification
3. Containment
4. Eradication
5. Recovery
6. Lessons Learned

Following this structured process helps organizations effectively manage and recover from cybersecurity incidents.

---

### Security Monitoring

SOC teams continuously monitor:

- Authentication logs
- Network traffic
- Endpoint activity
- Security events
- System logs

This continuous monitoring enables analysts to quickly identify and investigate suspicious behavior.

---

### Common SOC Tools

Several technologies commonly used in Security Operations Centers were introduced, including:

- Security Information and Event Management (SIEM)
- Endpoint Detection and Response (EDR)
- Intrusion Detection Systems (IDS)
- Threat Intelligence Platforms
- Log Management Solutions

These tools provide visibility into an organization's environment and support effective threat detection and response.

---

## Key Takeaways

- A SOC provides continuous monitoring against cyber threats.
- Security analysts investigate alerts and determine whether they represent genuine security incidents.
- Incident response follows a structured lifecycle to minimize impact and restore normal operations.
- SIEM platforms are central to collecting and analyzing security events.
- Effective teamwork and well-defined processes are essential for successful security operations.
