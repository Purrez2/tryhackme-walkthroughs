## Overview

The Welcome room serves as an introduction to the TryHackMe platform. It familiarizes users with the learning environment, navigation features, and the process of completing tasks and submitting answers.

Although this room does not involve technical exploitation or system enumeration, it provides a foundation for understanding how future cybersecurity challenges are structured on the platform.

---

## Objectives

The objectives of this room were to:

* Understand the TryHackMe learning environment.
* Learn how room tasks are organized.
* Practice answering questions within a room.
* Become familiar with the process of earning badges and tracking progress.

---

## Tasks Completed

### Task 1: Welcome to TryHackMe

Reviewed the introduction to the platform and learned how guided learning paths are structured.

**Key Takeaway:** TryHackMe provides hands-on cybersecurity training through interactive rooms covering multiple domains such as networking, web security, digital forensics, and penetration testing.

---

### Task 2: Navigating Rooms

Explored the room interface and learned how to move between tasks.

**Key Takeaway:** Each room is divided into sections containing instructions, questions, and practical exercises.

---

### Task 3: Answer Submission

Practiced submitting answers and verifying task completion.

**Key Takeaway:** Accurate documentation and attention to detail are important when completing cybersecurity exercises.

## Skills Developed

Although introductory, this room helped develop:
